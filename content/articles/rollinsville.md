Rollinsville is a small mountain town in the Colorado Rockies, a short drive from Boulder and Denver. It is a quiet place to start a trip, with trails, snow sports, fishing, and a handful of local businesses within a few minutes of the lodge.

## Outdoor Adventures

Rollinsville is a good base for the outdoors, whatever the season.

- **Hiking Trails:** Mud Lake and the West Magnolia Loop are popular starting points. For more options, read our [top hikes guide](/area-guide/articles/5_best_hikes_rollinsville).
- **Skiing and Snowboarding:** Eldora Mountain Resort is nearby. The Eldora Nordic Center has about 40 km of cross-country trails.
- **Fishing and Camping:** Streams and high-mountain lakes are close by. For specific spots, read our [fishing guide](/area-guide/articles/5_best_fishing_rollinsville).

## Cultural and Historical Significance

The town is named after John Q.A. Rollins. The Moffat Tunnel, completed in 1928, runs beneath the Continental Divide near town, and the railroad shaped much of the area's history.

- **Railroad Legacy:** the railroad that ran through town is part of its history.
- **Stage Stop Pub:** the [Stage Stop Pub](https://www.stagestoppub.com/) is temporarily under renovations.
- **Local Artisanal Scene:** Gold Dirt Distillery and [Howlin Wind Brewing & Blending](https://www.howlinwindbrewing.com/) are among the local producers.
- **U.S. Post Office:** the Rollinsville post office opened on January 31, 1871.
- **Peak to Peak Scenic Byway:** a scenic drive that passes through the area.

## Community Spirit

Local events and initiatives keep the town connected to visitors and residents alike.

- **Community Events:** seasonal events bring neighbors and visitors together.
- **Local Initiatives:** work currently underway includes:
  - Revitalization
  - Enhancing trail assets
  - A passenger railway stop

## Conclusion

Plan a day around the trails, the food, and the history, then come back to the lodge to rest. Rollinsville is worth a few days of exploring.
