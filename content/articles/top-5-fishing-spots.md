Fly fishing in the streams and trout in the lakes near Rollinsville make it one of the better fishing areas on the Front Range. Here are five spots to start with, counted down from fifth to first.

## 5. S. Boulder Creek

- **Location:** E. Portal Road and Boulder Canyon Road
- **Species:** rainbow, brown, and brook trout

A creek with easy access from the road and steady water for fly anglers.

## 4. Big Thompson River

- **Location:** Moraine Park, Rocky Mountain National Park

A river inside the national park. Check the current park regulations before you go.

## 3. Gross Reservoir

- **Location:** Coal Creek Canyon Road (CO-72)
- **Species:** rainbow, brown, and kokanee

A reservoir with a range of species. Check current regulations for other species present.

## 2. Clear Creek Canyon

- **Location:** US-6
- **Species:** brown and rainbow trout, with a few brook trout

A canyon stretch of water along US-6 that is easy to reach from town.

## 1. High Mountain Lakes

- **Location:** from the Moffat Tunnel trailhead into the James Peak Wilderness
- **Species:** rainbow trout

The hike in is the reward. These lakes are best reached later in the season.

## Tips for Fishing in Rollinsville

- **Seasonal Considerations**
  - Check the [seasonal fishing regulations and license fees](https://cpw.state.co.us/thingstodo/Pages/FishingSeasonDatesFees.aspx) before you go.
  - Run-off from the peaks swells creeks to almost unfishable levels between late April and late June.
  - High mountain lakes are usually not accessible until after June 21st, because of late-season snow drifts.
  - Early mornings and late evenings can be very cold in warmer months, with nights in the high 30s at high elevations. Bring layers.
- **Gear Up**
  - Pack rods, reels, and a variety of lures or flies.
  - Bring rain gear. Temperatures can swing by more than 10 degrees during storms.
- **Respect Nature**
  - Practice Leave No Trace principles, and keep your distance from wildlife.

Pack your gear, check conditions before you leave, and enjoy the water.
