Rollinsville itself has no dedicated craft store, but the towns nearby have plenty of options for scrapbookers. If you're bringing a project to the lodge, these are the places to stock up.

## Finding Scrapbooking Supplies

### Closest Proximity To Rollinsville

These shops are the closest options to the lodge:

- [Michaels Market Square](https://maps.app.goo.gl/56DiQh69N17uMRvY6)
- [Two Hands Paperie](https://maps.app.goo.gl/X3rBHfpCPCJskD3C7)
- [Joann Fabric and Crafts](https://maps.app.goo.gl/kP6AgHWcNeX6Phkk6)
- [Art Parts Creative Reuse Center](https://maps.app.goo.gl/9P1BXRYMeNRRw1Gz6)

## Capturing Memories While Building Memories

Bring your supplies to the lodge, set up at the table, and document the trip as you go. Rollinsville makes a good backdrop.
