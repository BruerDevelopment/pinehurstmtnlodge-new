The area around Rollinsville has trails for beginners and experienced hikers, with mountain lakes, forest loops, and wide views. These five are a good place to start.

## Mud Lake - Boulder County

A lakeside hike in Boulder County, with views across the water.

## Caribou Ranch - Boulder County

Another Boulder County trail with scenic views, a short drive from town.

## West Magnolia Loop Trail

A popular loop trail near Rollinsville.

## Front Range Trailhead - USDA Forest Service

A trailhead managed by the USDA Forest Service and a good starting point for hikes into the Front Range.

## Raccoon Trail

A trail near Nederland.

Lace up your hiking boots, pack some water and snacks, and embark on your next adventure in Rollinsville!
