Rollinsville mixes outdoor adventure with local food, drink, and small-town culture. These five stops are a good way to spend an afternoon between trips.

## Gold Dirt Distillery

A local distillery and one of the town's artisanal producers. Visit the [Gold Dirt Distillery website](https://www.golddirtdistillery.com/) for current offerings.

Hours of Operation: Wed-Thur 4-8ish, Fri-Sat 3-9ish, Sun 3-8ish

## Toss: Wood Fired Eatery

A wood-fired kitchen in town. See the [Toss website](https://www.tosswoodfired.com/) for the menu.

Hours of Operation: Wed/Thur/Fri 3-8, Sat/Sun 12-8

## Howlin Wind Brewing & Blending

A brewery and blending house. See the [Howlin Wind website](https://www.howlinwindbrewing.com/) for beers and events.

Hours of Operation: Sun 1-8, Mon/Wed/Thur 2-8, Tue 4-9, Fri 2-9, Sat 1-9

## Melt Coffee Microroastery & Espresso Bar

A small-batch coffee roaster and espresso bar. Follow [Melt on Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/meltcoffeebar/?hl=en) for current hours and specials.

Hours of Operation: Mon/Thur/Fri 8-3, Sat/Sun 8-5

## Moffat Tunnel

The tunnel is a landmark in its own right and a reminder of the area's railroad history. It is open at all times, but it is best seen in daylight.

Hours of Operation: Open 24 / 7, but best in the daylight!

Pick a few of these, plan your day around them, and come back to the lodge when you're done.
